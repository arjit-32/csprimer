Identity and Access Management (IAM) basics in OCI.

### IAM Components:
- **Compartments**: Organizing resources.
- **Tenancy**: The whole root of the account.
- **Policies**: SQL-like statements for access.

```sql
-- Example Policy
Allow group Developers to manage all-resources in compartment Dev_Env
```
