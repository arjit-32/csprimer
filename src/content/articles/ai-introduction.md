Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to the development of computer systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, problem-solving, perception, and decision-making

## What is AI?

AI is broadly defined as the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines. This includes:

- Learning: Acquiring information and rules for using it.
- Reasoning: Using rules to reach approximate or definite conclusions.
- Self-correction: Improving performance over time.

**Types of AI:**

Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI):  It excels at specific, well-defined tasks but lacks broader understanding or adaptability.

Artificial General Intelligence (AGI): Also known as Strong AI or human-level AI, AGI would match or surpass human cognitive abilities across a wide range of tasks, including learning new skills without task-specific reprogramming.

Artificial Superintelligence (ASI): A hypothetical future stage where AI surpasses human intelligence

**History and Key Milestones**

The journey of AI spans decades, marked by periods of optimism ("AI summers") and setbacks ("AI winters").

| **Year** | **Milestone** | **Description** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1950 | Turing Test proposed | Alan Turing introduces the idea of machines thinking. |
| 1956 | Dartmouth Conference | Birth of AI as a field; John McCarthy coins "Artificial Intelligence." |
| 1960s-70s | Early programs like ELIZA | Chatbots and expert systems emerge; first AI winter due to overhype. |
| 1997 | Deep Blue defeats Kasparov | IBM's chess AI beats world champion. |
| 2011 | Watson wins Jeopardy! | IBM Watson demonstrates natural language understanding. |
| 2012 | AlexNet revolutionizes image recognition | Deep learning breakthrough with convolutional neural networks. |
| 2016 | AlphaGo defeats Go champion | DeepMind's AI masters complex strategy game. |
| 2018 | GPT-1 and rise of transformers | Foundation for modern large language models. |
| 2020 | GPT-3 released | Massive scale enables few-shot learning. |
| 2022 | ChatGPT launches | Generative AI goes mainstream, reaching 100M users quickly. |
| 2023-2024 | Multimodal models (e.g., GPT-4) | AI handles text, images, and video. |
| 2025 | Advanced reasoning models & agents | Models like GPT-5 show PhD-level reasoning; agentic AI matures for real-world tasks. |

**What is Data Science?**
Data Science is broader than ML/AI. It combines domain knowledge, programming, statistics, and visualization to extract actionable insights from data. Data Scientists may build ML models but also focus on business understanding, data cleaning (often 80% of the work!), and communicating results.
